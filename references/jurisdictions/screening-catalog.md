## Saudi Arabia — PDPL

DPIA-like assessment expected for high-risk processing under the PDPL (Royal Decree M/19,
2021, as amended) and SDAIA's implementing regulations; GDPR-shaped but with consent as the
dominant basis and a transfer regime run through SDAIA approvals/exceptions. Regulator: SDAIA.
Arabic authoritative text. *(2026-11-21: the Apr–May 2025 draft amendments were **not
adopted**; SDAIA published a **revised draft** of the Implementing Regulations and consulted
on it **2026-10-06 → 2026-11-05** (Istitlaa; SPA release N2308486). The draft would
restructure and widen the **Art. 25 DPIA triggers** — adding large-scale processing,
children's data without a scale qualifier, and processing using "emerging technologies" —
and add a general data-localisation requirement, tightened breach notification, controller
registration and the DPO rules folded into the Regulations. Not in force; the 2023
Implementing Regulations and their Art. 25 triggers still apply; entry into force would be
60 days after Gazette publication. SDAIA is actively enforcing (dozens of published violation
decisions) and issued an AI Adoption Framework (Nov 2025).)*

## UAE — Federal PDPL

Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021 requires a DPIA (recalled Art. 21) for high-risk processing,
notably new technologies and large-scale sensitive data — but the **executive regulations
remain pending**; verify status before asserting anything operative. *(**Correction
2026-11-21:** the 2026-08-11 / 2026-09-12 note that the Executive Regulations "were issued in
2026 by Cabinet decision and are being enforced" is **withdrawn**. Several sources that
checked the UAE Legislation portal (uaelegislation.gov.ae) on 18–19 November 2026 found **no
Executive Regulations and no Art. 26 penalties decision** for Decree-Law 45/2021; the
Cabinet decision numbers circulating in practitioner guides do not correspond to any PDPL
instrument (one, Cabinet Resolution 59/2026, is a competition-law act). Consequently the
PDPL's six-month compliance window under Art. 29 has not started and there is no number to
cite. Supervision is transitioning to a new **Artificial Intelligence and Data Authority**
(Cabinet approval 2026-06-14, absorbing the UAE Data Office), which is expected to finalise
the Regulations. The Art. 21 DPIA duty exists in the Decree-Law itself, unchanged. Confirm
on the portal before every UAE-scope run.)*
Free-zone regimes (DIFC DP Law 2020, ADGM DPR 2021) have their own, more mature, DPIA
expectations and their own regulators — check which body of law actually applies to the
establishment. *(2026-11-21: DIFC Consultation Paper No. 3 of 2026 — Regulation 10 on
autonomous and semi-autonomous systems, an Autonomous Systems Officer role, a new Regulation
11 on certification schemes — closed 2026-07-18; enactment unconfirmed. ADGM: no 2026
amendment beyond the 2025 Substantial Public Interest Conditions Rules.)*

## South Africa — POPIA

No express DPIA duty in POPIA itself; regulation 4(1)(b) under s. 55 requires the information
officer to ensure a "personal information impact assessment" is done. Prior authorisation from
the Information Regulator is required for enumerated processing categories (s. 57–58 —
unique identifiers, criminal data, credit reporting, transborder transfers of special personal
information of children) — a genuine stop-the-line mechanism closer to Art. 36 than most.

## Nigeria — NDPA 2023

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 requires a DPIA where processing is likely to result in
high risk (recalled s. 28), and the NDPC's General Application and Implementation Directive
(GAID, 2025) operationalizes it; data controllers "of major importance" carry registration and
audit obligations through licensed DPCOs. Regulator: NDPC. *(2026-08-11: GAID confirmed in
force — issued ~2025-03-20, **effective 2025-09-19**; the NDPR 2019 and its 2020
Implementation Framework no longer apply.)*

## Japan — APPI

No DPIA obligation; the PPC encourages PIAs as best practice and expects records of
third-party provision, purpose limitation, and anonymized/pseudonymized information handling.
Japan holds mutual adequacy with the EU — relevant to the EU limb's transfer row, not to a
Japanese assessment duty. *(2026-08-11, re-checked 2026-11-21: the APPI amendment from the
triennial review (Law No. 56 of 2026) **passed the Diet 2026-07-10, promulgated 2026-07-17,
not yet in force** — the cabinet order setting the main commencement date has not been
issued (deadline 2028-07-16; penalty-related provisions in force 2027-01-17) — direct
administrative monetary penalties (課徴金), under-16 children's data, biometric data,
AI/statistical-use consent relaxation, vendor oversight; still no statutory PIA mandate.)*

## Thailand — PDPA

GDPR-shaped statute (2019, fully effective 2022) with a developing secondary-regulation
layer. **DPIAs are not generally mandatory** — no standalone mandatory-DPIA PDPC notification
was found by the 2026-08-11 pass (do not assert one); DPIA expectations arrive via guidance,
notably the PDPC's AI-related guidelines (effective 2026-03-25) calling for DPIAs for
high-risk AI, and the **PDPC AI personal-data guidelines v1.0 published in the Royal Gazette
2026-11-02** (Vol. 143, Special Section 261 ง; expressly non-binding; expect a DPIA before
high-risk AI projects such as rights-affecting automated decisions or training on sensitive
data — `[web search — verify]`, 2026-11-21). Also: DPO now mandatory for state agencies (Oct
2025); first major PDPC fines issued Aug 2025; data access/copy rules in force 2026-09-14.
Verify the current notification set before asserting any mandatory trigger — none existed as
of 2026-11-21.

## Mexico — LFPDPPP (2025) / LGPDPPSO

A new **LFPDPPP** (private sector) was published 2025-03-20 and took effect 2025-03-21,
abrogating the 2010 law and dissolving INAI (supervision moved to the Secretaría
Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno, which is actively running verification procedures);
**amended by decree of 2025-11-14** (harmonisation with the national civil-procedure code —
not substantive). **No general private-sector PIA obligation** surfaced — screen for
security-measures and ARCO-rights obligations instead, and verify against the statute text:
**no new Reglamento has been published** (the 90-day deadline lapsed; the 2011 Reglamento
applies suppletorily where not contradictory). The public-sector **LGPDPPSO** (also amended
2025-11-14) does require an **evaluación de impacto** submitted 30 days before deploying or
materially modifying public-facing systems — relevant only where the client is or serves a
Mexican public body. *(Added 2026-08-11; re-checked 2026-11-21.)*

## Chile — Ley 21.719 (entry into force in flux — check the date on every run)

Chile's new data protection law (published 2024-12-13) creates an **Agencia de Protección de
Datos Personales** and a GDPR-shaped regime with a **prior data-protection impact assessment
for high-risk processing** (large-scale processing, profiling, sensitive data and similar
triggers — cite the article only after a fetch). **Statutory entry into force is
2026-12-01.** A government bill postponing it to **2027-10-15** (and enlarging the Agency's
board from three to five) passed the **Senate on 2026-10-28** and the **Chamber of Deputies
around 2026-11-17**, and was dispatched for promulgation on 2026-11-20; **publication in the
Diario Oficial had not been confirmed as of 2026-11-21**. If it is not published before 1
December 2026 the law is in force on that date; if it is, nothing bites until October 2027.
State which branch applies, with the date checked, in any Chile-scope cover note. Regulator:
the Agency (once constituted). `[web search — verify]` (camara.cl and senado.cl pages seen
2026-11-21)

## Sri Lanka — Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022

A commencement order gazetted around 2026-07-22 brings the substantive Parts into operation
on **2027-01-01**; Part VII (administrative penalties) is not yet commenced. The Act requires
a **DPIA before high-risk processing** (recalled s. 24 — verify) and a DPO for designated
controllers. Regulator: Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka. Not yet operative; flag as
incoming for long-lived processing with Sri Lankan data subjects. `[web search — verify]`
(2026-11-21; gazette not fetched)
