## 1. Instrument and statute

- **Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection, Art. 34:** the controller must
  conduct a personal data protection impact assessment where processing has a **high
  potential risk** to data subjects. `[web search — verify]`
- **GR 33/2026 Art. 120 — DPIA triggers (seven categories):** the implementing regulation
  enumerates seven trigger categories for the Art. 34 duty, with **no fixed numeric
  thresholds** — volume, sensitivity and duration are weighing factors, not bright lines —
  and a **new-technology trigger that expressly includes AI/ML and IoT**. The list tracks
  the statute's outline (sensitive data; large-scale processing; systematic evaluation,
  scoring or monitoring; matching or merging of datasets; new technology; processing
  limiting data subjects' rights) and visibly descends from WP29's criteria.
  `[web search — verify]`
- **GR 33/2026 Art. 121 — timing and content:** the DPIA must be performed **before
  processing begins**, with prescribed content (description of processing and purposes;
  necessity/proportionality; risk assessment for data subjects' rights; protective
  measures) — Art. 35(7)-shaped, so the GDPR spine over-satisfies it. **DPO consultation
  on the DPIA is codified.** `[web search — verify]`
- Regulator: the PDP supervisory institution under the President (formation phase);
  Komdigi handles the interim. Verify the current authority per run.

## 3. Divergence notes

Section 6 deltas where they change the answer: dual-basis structure (consent plus
enumerated lawful bases); **specific personal data** includes health, biometric,
genetic, criminal record, child data, personal financial data; children's processing
requires parental consent; cross-border transfer requires the destination to have
comparable-or-higher protection, appropriate safeguards, or consent (Arts. 55–56
pattern). All `[model knowledge — verify]`; GR 33/2026 elaborates several of these —
check its transfer and children's-data chapters before relying on the statutory pattern
alone. `[web search — verify]`

## 4. Privilege posture

Indonesian advocate confidentiality exists but privilege doctrine is undeveloped for
in-house work product; treat the DPIA record as factual and hold candid analysis in the
privileged spine, especially while the authority's production powers are undefined.
